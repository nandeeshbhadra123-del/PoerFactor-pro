02 / THE METHOD

The science behind the result.

Understand how the calculator estimates the correction requirement.

01

Determine the phase angle

Power factor is the cosine of the phase angle between voltage and current.

φ = cos⁻¹(PF)
02

Calculate reactive power

Estimate reactive power for the existing and desired power factor at the same real power.

Q = P × tan(φ)
03

Estimate compensation

The difference between the two reactive power values estimates capacitor-bank capacity.

Qc = Q₁ − Q₂
THE COMPLETE FORMULA

Required capacitor capacity

Qc = P [tan(cos⁻¹ PF₁) − tan(cos⁻¹ PF₂)]
03 / WHY IT MATTERS

Less reactive burden.
Better utilisation.

Improving a lagging power factor can reduce reactive power demand and supply current for the same real power, potentially freeing capacity in electrical equipment.

Engineering note

This calculator provides theoretical estimates for sinusoidal systems and assumes constant real power. Actual installations require assessment of harmonics, resonance, load variation, capacitor-bank steps, switching, and protection by a qualified electrical engineer. Results are not a substitute for an engineering design.
